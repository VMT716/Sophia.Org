Our Story

North Star Bakery began with a dream.

When two IT coworkers, fed up with the daily grind, joined forces.
They came up with a plan to build a sustainable discount bakery.
Driven by passion, North Star Bakery always strives to make quality it's North Star.

Sourcing Philosophy

By choosing to source our ingredients locally, we control our quality.

Our promise that every ingredient, if possible is sourced from local partners and sources.
